sim-from-standard

Three simulations, each built from a standard and aimed at one misconception.

CCSS 5.NF.B.5

Scale Machine

“Multiplying always makes bigger.” Slide a multiplier below 1 and watch the stick shrink.
CCSS 7.SP.A.1

Jar of Beads

“A small sample mirrors the population.” Draw hundreds of handfuls and see how wide the truth hides.
NGSS MS-LS4-6

Moth Forest

“Organisms change because they need to.” No moth changes colour, yet the forest does.
