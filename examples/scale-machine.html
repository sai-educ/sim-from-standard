Teacher note

Standard: 5.NF.B.5 – interpret multiplication as scaling (resizing) by comparing the size of a product to the size of one factor.

Target misconception: “Multiplication always makes things bigger.” Learners generalise from whole-number work.

Ask: What number makes the stick the same length? Where does the answer switch from shrinking to growing? What would ×0 do?

Extend: predict the product for ×¾ and ×1¼ before moving the slider; connect to unit rates and percent change.

Limit: shows lengths only; area models of fraction multiplication are not covered.
