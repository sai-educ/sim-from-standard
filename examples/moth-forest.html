Teacher note

Standard: MS-LS4-6 – use mathematical representations to support explanations of how natural selection may lead to increases and decreases of specific traits in populations over time.

Target misconception: “Organisms change because they need to” (need-driven, Lamarckian reasoning). Here no moth ever changes colour: the picture only shifts because who survives and reproduces shifts.

Ask: Did any one moth change colour? What would happen if bark darkened faster than variation could appear? Why does the average move gradually?

Extend: set mutation to zero in the code and see what is lost.

Limit: a single heritable trait with random mating and no genetics; real colour traits involve many genes.
